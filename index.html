About the workshop

This workshop is aimed at graduate students and postdocs working on gravitational-wave data analysis who want practical, working knowledge of ringdown inference. Sessions move from theory to pipeline internals to a hands-on analysis of real LVK data, using pyRing to extract quasi-normal mode frequencies and damping times and to test their consistency with the Kerr hypothesis.

No prior pyRing experience is required. Participants should bring a laptop with Python installed; setup instructions will be circulated ahead of the workshop.

Program

  • DAY 1
    Ringdown theory & black hole spectroscopy
    Quasi-normal modes, the no-hair theorem, and what ringdown tests can and can't tell us about Kerr.
  • DAY 2
    Inside pyRing
    Pipeline architecture, priors, waveform models, and a guided walkthrough of a real O4 configuration.
  • DAY 3
    Hands-on analysis session
    Participants run their own ringdown fit end to end, on cluster resources provided for the workshop.
